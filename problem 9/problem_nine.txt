a = m ** 2 - n ** 2
b = 2 * m * n
c = m ** 2 + n ** 2

a + b + c = 1000
m ** 2 - n ** 2 + 2 * m * n + m ** 2 + n ** 2 = 1000
m ** 2 + m * n = 500
m * (m + n) = 500

breaking down 500:
1. 2, 250
2. 2, 2, 125
3. 2, 2, 5, 25

2 * 2 * 5 = 20
and 25 = 20 + 5
So, m = 20 and n = 5
